About

A trainer who builds — with AI as the workshop.

I'm Antony Addy, a certified bilingual (English / French) trainer based in the South of France. For years I've built the tools my own learners needed; today I design and build them for others too.

Using AI in the loop, I move from a rough idea to a polished, deployed site remarkably fast — without losing the craft. Clean design, clear language in the tongue your audience thinks in, and products that are genuinely nice to use. Teaching, hospitality, sport, professional services: each one gets a site that fits it.

If you can describe it, there's a good chance I can build it — and have it live sooner than you'd expect.
